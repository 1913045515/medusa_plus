Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains how Wolzq collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you use www.wolzq.com and related services.

1Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information we collect through our website, storefront, support channels, digital product delivery systems, customer accounts, and related interactions with Wolzq.

It does not apply to third-party websites, payment providers, hosting partners, or services that maintain their own privacy notices.

2Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

👤

Account Information

Name, email address, password hash, and related account profile details.

🛒

Transaction Information

Orders, payment status, billing country or region, course purchases, and delivery records.

📬

Support Communications

Messages you send to support, questions about purchases, and related attachments.

🎓

Learning Activity

Lesson access records, course progress data, and related gated-content access logs.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, browser type, device type, and operating system.
  • Pages viewed, referring URLs, timestamps, interactions with storefront pages, and error logs.
  • Session and authentication data stored through secure cookies or similar technologies, including account session state.
  • Technical information required to secure downloads, gated course playback, and account access.

2.3 Payment Information

Payment transactions are processed by third-party payment providers such as PayPal. Wolzq does not store full credit card numbers, card security codes, or bank account passwords on its own servers.

3Sources of Information

We collect personal information from the following sources:

  • Directly from you when you create an account, place an order, contact support, or access paid content.
  • Automatically from your browser, device, and interactions with our Services.
  • From payment processors regarding transaction status, fraud screening, and order confirmation.
  • From infrastructure and security providers that help us host, secure, and monitor the Services.

7Security Safeguards

We maintain a written and operational security approach designed to protect personal information and digital assets from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

🔐

Encrypted Transport

HTTPS and TLS are used to protect data in transit between your browser and our Services.

🔑

Credential Protection

Passwords are stored in hashed form and administrative access is restricted on a least-privilege basis.

🛡️

Monitoring and Logging

We log authentication and system events to support fraud detection, incident response, and system integrity.

📁

Controlled Delivery

Protected files and course media may be served through gated routes, signed URLs, or access-controlled delivery patterns.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at security@wolzq.com.

10Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in a manner subject to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact privacy@wolzq.com and we will take appropriate steps to review and delete the information where required.

12Do Not Track and Sale/Sharing Disclosures

Wolzq does not sell personal information for monetary consideration and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We also do not knowingly process sensitive personal information for purposes that would trigger a state-law right to limit such use, except as necessary to provide the Services.
